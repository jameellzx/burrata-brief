V1"The burrata cut" (hero craving ad)
The burrata is torn open and the cream spills over the tomato, filmed so close you can hear it.
Hooks ① no text, just the tear (first frame = knife touching burrata) ② "هالجبن لازم ينأكل نفس اليوم" ③ "Burrata Caprese · 2.850 · ٣ الفجر بعد"
O2 Complete hospitalityO3 Baked overnight C1, C2, C3-d, C7 · Options O3, O2 · Formats A1, A2, A5 (#15, #29) · replaces Q1 as the opening shot of the burrata series
Hooks, beats, filming
Beats (8 s) 0–1 s knife tip touches the burrata · 1–3 s slow tear, cream spills onto tomato (120 fps) · 3–5 s olive oil + basil drop · 5–7 s hands close the sourdough, press, cut in half: cross-section · 7–8 s hold + name & price
Shots macro tear (side backlight), 45° spill, top-down oil drop, cross-section held to lens

Cut-downs 5 s loop (tear only), 15 s with Burrata Honey and Burrata Berry versions (honey pour, berries burst), carousel F8 (ingredient blocks).
