W

Winners

50 KD/month
Objective
Sales · Purchase (scale)
Who
All Kuwait, Advantage+ audience
Offer
The best ads from every campaign
  1. Every week, check each campaign.
  2. Ads with the lowest cost per result and the best numbers move to Winners.
  3. Move them with the same post ID, so likes and comments stay.
  4. Winners gets more money: start at 50 KD, then shift 10–20% from the weakest campaign.
  5. Wait ~7 days before judging any ad (learning phase).

Rules

  • Boxes do not include coffee: say "add coffee in the same order".
  • No real customers on camera: models or hands only.
  • No competitor names or logos.
  • No discounts.
  • No "100% butter" until the butter type is checked.
  • Promise wording waits for brand approval.
  • No invented reviews or quotes.

Organic only
