Research summary

Use it when creating ads to know messaging and positioning.

This research was done on reviews, real people and real comments. Quotes are word for word; S# = source number in the full research file.

👥 Who

Kuwaitis drive the café scene; expats are 70% of the country and mostly working-age men — the night/early-shift crowd S40S16.

Burrata's own reviewers: young Kuwaitis (lots of women), espresso nerds, Saudi visitors who came from an influencer, nearby hospital/office people.

“مكان هادي وبعيد عن زحمة القهاوي”S4

✨ Dreams

A quiet spot that feels like theirs — "a space for people to simply be" S38. Late, tidy, calm.

“مكان هادي ومرتب بوقت متأخر”S19
“It can be your dose of peacefulness.”S18

😣 Pain

KD 2+ for decor and hype; same coffee, different theme.

“The aesthetic of the place almost justifies the mediocre menu”S11
“Any espresso or americano over 1 KD is absolute robbery”S14

Fake "24 hours": night shift is a worse café.

“حاطين إنهم 24 ساعة ، جيناهم الساعة 5:50 صباحاً الموظفين يقولون مانقدم إلا 6”S22

🎯 Blame

Greedy "Instagram" cafés raising prices every opening; untrained staff; no manager at night.

“coffee place owners are bumping the price of a simple latte with every new opening”S14
“informed there is no Mather at night shift”S22

🧠 Beliefs / prejudices

"Specialty" and "fresh" are just labels. Good quiet spots get ruined or close.

“same swill as the next guy, just with a different theme”S12
“If i tell them then it won't be quiet anymore..”S10

📖 Best story

"كوخ بوراتا" — viewers named the place "the cottage" themselves; it's the most-viewed viewer clip S6.

Burrata cheese — born on a farm in Puglia when snow stopped a farmer reaching market; made from leftovers too good to waste; "supposed to be eaten on the same day it's made" S34.

Old Kuwaiti qahwa — modest wooden benches, regulars, stories of ships S37. Burrata = that, open to everyone, all night.

🏪 Competitors

24h in the city centre: % Arabica Downtown, Yardek (≈1 km), 20 Grams S21S22. Yardek has the night-shift horror stories.

Bakeries close 10pm–midnight (Layersmaster, Juna, Bakehaus, Happy Crescent, Stories) S33S24.

Nobody names a butter, a bake schedule or a roaster. Nobody gives a guarantee. Burrata is 0.7–0.8 KD cheaper than NAC on V60 / Spanish latte S26.

"Burrata" as an ingredient isn't exclusive (Layersmaster, Sansho sell burrata items) S25S27.

⚙️ Mechanism candidates

Problem: the "fake 24h" café (night shift = second-class) · frozen factory pastry (up to 80% in French bakeries S41) · decor over substance.

Solution: "the cottage that never closes — same standard at 3am" · "baked here, through the night" (brand must confirm) · three single-origin espressos at KD 1.500 (reviewers love the Colombian "cherry" one S4) · "made today, eaten today" (burrata logic).

⚠️ Warnings (from Burrata's own reviews [S4][S3])

No toilet, no prayer room, limited parking, small room, weak AC, hot outside in summer, some smells, a 15-min counter wait, a price-change complaint ignored on WhatsApp. Copy must not over-promise "stay all night and study".

V60 at 2.5 KD called "too light" — coffee opinions split; espresso is the strong card.

🕳 Gaps

Nothing public on founders, baker, roaster, or why "Burrata". Earlier branch in Sabhan (one reviewer) / South Surra (unverified).

B2B + catering terms unknown (Instagram highlights need login).

Is the bakery baked in-house overnight? This decides the main mechanism. Update: yes, confirmed by the brand.

No reviewer mentions the 24h/late-night angle yet → it's unused.

How it works

  1. Every week, check each C3 ad set (and the other campaigns).
  2. The ads with the lowest cost and the best results move to Winners.
  3. Each winner runs in both: Winners · Sales (site) and Winners · WhatsApp.
  4. Move them with the same post ID, so likes and comments stay.
  5. Winners get more money over time: shift 10–20% from the weakest campaign.
  6. Wait ~7 days before judging any ad (learning phase).
